Priority Proposals

Proposal 1: South-East Skills Mobility Partnership Hub

A dual-track skills, language and certification pipeline at Ogbonnaya Onu Polytechnic preparing youth for employment at home and ethical mobility abroad, a triple win for your labour market and our communities

Download
Proposal 2: Centre for Migration, Skills Mobility and Development at MOUAU

A permanent Migration Resource Centre, certified German/French language expansion, and a migration research unit, an institutional legacy with your partnership written into it

Download
Proposal 3: Public Awareness & CSO Capacity-Building Programme

region-wide, mission-verified information on skills demand, visa policies and reintegration offers, making every South-East youth an informed applicant, and every CSO a permanent information officer

Download
Proposal 4: South-East Migration Data & Evidence System

the data architecture serving the emerging Abia State Migration Agency and the Southeast Migration Working Group; governance that counts

Download
Proposal 5: Returnee Rehabilitation & Reintegration Programme, Abia

Download

Climate-smart skills and a mobility observatory anchored in a university of agriculture, tackling migration at its roots, in line with Southeast Migration Dialogue Priority 9

Africa's flagship dialogue on legal protection, institutional recourse and state accountability in the face of xenophobic violence
